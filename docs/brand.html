Year One
Months 1–12

Findable

Year one is findable, remembered, and delivered.

  • The search pilot, with a baseline before anything changes.
  • An email list from market day and from orders.
  • A photo library and a short account of the people.
  • The Compact project on every page that sells meat. Named as a project.
  • Thanksgiving delivery inside 200 miles, on the six corridors.
Year Two
Months 13–24

Repeat

Year two turns a holiday buyer into someone who comes back.

  • Eggs, seasonal boxes, and the holidays, more than once a year.
  • Wholesale only with kitchens that are already asking, and only if the processor can feed it.
  • A simple egg or meat share once the margin is real.
  • A note from the farm when there is something to say.
  • Visits and the internship if the family wants guests.
Year Three
Months 25–36

Capacity

Year three is when repeat orders carry the year.

  • A second processor only if the current date is the bottleneck.
  • Help with the work only after repeat orders can carry it.
  • The internship named as a path into the work.
  • Another product after the meat boxes are already repeating.

Years Four and Five
Months 37–60

Earned, or Not

These years are not a plan. They are what the first three make possible.

  • A wider map only if repeat orders and the processor can take more.
  • A Nashville presence is its own decision.
  • Succession and the land stay on the family's clock.
  • Soil tests go public when the farm wants that proof public.
