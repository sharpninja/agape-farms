Three Pillars

Three Pillars

Land

Documented Stewardship

The Cumberland River Compact's managed-grazing project documents a specific conservation effort. Discuss grazing, animal care, and measured outcomes accurately; no unverified certification claims.

People

Repeat Relationships

Market buyers, neighbors, educators, faith communities, and delivery customers can become repeat customers through reliable availability and clear ordering.

Purpose

Faith-Driven Service

Honor the farm's Christian commitment to stewardship and community in both the stories it tells and the customer experience it provides.

Year One
Months 1–12

Foundation

Year one creates a measurable foundation; the delivery pilot is a separate decision.

  • Complete the approved SEO pilot, preserve a Search Console baseline, and review results at 30, 60, and 90 days.
  • Gather opt-in email subscriptions with clear consent and a working unsubscribe option.
  • Create an approved farm photo library and concise founder story with permission from the people pictured.
  • Link the verified Cumberland River Compact managed-grazing project without asserting an unverified certification.
  • Run only approved holiday delivery pilots that pass product, compliance, capacity, cold-chain, and margin checks.
Year Two
Months 13–24

Prove Repeat Demand

Return customers matter more than a channel count.

  • Test recurring pickup or ordering arrangements only for products with dependable availability.
  • Evaluate a meat-share or seasonal box using measured contribution margin and customer interest.
  • Investigate wholesale opportunities only after confirming processing, packaging, pricing, and fulfillment economics.
  • Send useful, permission-based farm updates at a cadence the family can sustain.
  • Consider educational visits and internships only if capacity, safety, and family preference allow.
Year Three
Months 25–36

Expand Selectively

Expansion follows evidence, not a date on the calendar.

  • Add another processor only if a measured capacity bottleneck and unit economics justify it.
  • Add staff or contract help only when recurring contribution covers the cost.
  • Support educational opportunities with clear responsibilities and available supervision.
  • Pilot adjacent products after demand for core items is demonstrated.
  • Set minimum margin and operational-capacity thresholds before opening any new channel.
Years Four & Five
Months 37–60 · Conditional

Preserve Options

No additional geography, facility, or sales channel is preapproved.

  • Let retention, cash generation, and farm profitability determine whether wider service areas are justified.
  • Treat a Nashville wholesale or pickup presence as a separate investment decision.
  • Keep land access, family workload, and succession planning under the farm's control.
  • Publish soil, habitat, or grazing measures only with validated methods and the farm's approval.
What Sustainability Means

What Sustainability Means

Ecological

Improve grazing, forage recovery, and water protection; document specific projects and measured results where evidence exists.

Economic

Require each sales channel to cover its incremental costs and justify its use of scarce farm capacity.

Purpose

Make faith commitments, educational work, and community relationships part of operations rather than unsupported marketing claims.

The roadmap remains adjustable as conditions and documented results change.
