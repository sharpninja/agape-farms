02
Weeks 2–4 · Local Presence

Represent Your Farm Locally

  • Confirm which Google Business Profile belongs to your operation and whether its access and details are correct.
  • Review your business name, category, contact details, website, public location or service area, hours, and genuine pickup options.
  • Describe your Adams-area farm and Clarksville market presence accurately without advertising delivery areas you have not approved.
  • Invite customers to leave honest, voluntary reviews; do not script their wording or offer incentives.
  • Answer the questions your customers ask about reservations, deposits, variable weights, pickup windows, and contact.
03
Months 2–3 · Farm Stories

Tell Your Story With Evidence

  • Work with you on a concise account of your team, stewardship priorities, and educational or community work.
  • Use photographs and descriptions you have reviewed to explain your grazing and animal-care practices.
  • Reference the Cumberland River Compact managed-grazing case study for that project, without presenting certification as verified.
  • Measure how people search for your products and locations before labeling any keyword an opportunity.
