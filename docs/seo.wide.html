01

On the Page

  • Search Console baseline first: queries, clicks, indexed URLs.
  • Rewrite titles and descriptions. You sign the copy, then it goes live.
  • Separate the deposit from the price and fix the turkey typo.
  • Headings, image descriptions, and LocalBusiness schema on the pages that sell.
Success is the agreed pages live and measured. Not a promised ranking.
02

The Local Listing

  • The Google profile that belongs to the farm: name, category, hours, pickup, site.
  • Adams and Clarksville. Not a delivery area the farm hasn't published.
  • Ask for the review after a real sale. Don't script it.
03

The Story

  • A short piece on the people and the grazing, in the farm's photos.
  • The Compact project named as a project.
  • Read the queries after the pages change. Call something an opportunity only when the numbers say so.
