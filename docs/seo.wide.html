What Needs Attention

Where to Improve

Search Result Titles

The public Team page appears as 'Team 1 — Agape Farmstead.' Replace generic titles and review each priority page's actual title and meta description before claiming any metadata is absent.

Ordering Clarity

Products mix per-pound rates with deposits and final-weight calculations. Label units and payment terms separately. Correct 'Whole Turkey Depsoit' after confirming farm-approved checkout wording.

Local Relevance

Clarify the Adams-area farm and its Clarksville market presence near the primary content. Verify the business profile, canonical URLs, sitemap, structured data, and image alternatives before marking them defective.

01
Weeks 1–2 · Verify & Implement

Build a Measurable Foundation

  • Capture Search Console and analytics baselines — queries, impressions, clicks, indexed URLs, and priority-page visits.
  • Inventory real titles, meta descriptions, headings, canonicals, robots/sitemap behavior, images, and structured data; retain evidence for each finding.
  • Propose page-specific titles and descriptions for the home, products, team, and turkey reservation pages; implement only after farm approval.
  • Clarify per-pound prices, deposits, final-weight charges, pickup and delivery arrangements, and ordering FAQs without changing approved commercial terms.
  • Improve meaningful headings, internal links, descriptive image alternatives, and validated structured data only where the business facts support them.
Acceptance: agreed pages updated, technical tests documented, and baseline captured. A ranking increase in two weeks is not guaranteed.
02
Weeks 2–4 · Local Presence

Verify the Listing

  • Confirm ownership and the correct Google Business Profile before asserting that a listing exists, is incomplete, or needs to be created.
  • Verify business name, category, contact details, website, public location or service area, hours, and actual pickup options.
  • Explain Adams-area farming and Clarksville market participation accurately; do not imply unapproved delivery coverage.
  • Invite voluntary, authentic customer reviews without incentives or coached keywords.
  • Publish practical answers on reservations, deposits, weight-based prices, fulfillment windows, and contact channels.
03
Months 2–3 · Farm Stories

Show the Work Behind the Food

  • Publish a concise, verified story of the farmers and their agricultural and educational work.
  • Explain grazing and animal-care practices using farm-approved photographs and precise, factual language.
  • Link to the Cumberland River Compact managed-grazing case study as evidence of that specific project—not as proof of certification.
  • Test product-and-location search queries using Search Console, rather than declaring any keyword 'winnable' without data.
